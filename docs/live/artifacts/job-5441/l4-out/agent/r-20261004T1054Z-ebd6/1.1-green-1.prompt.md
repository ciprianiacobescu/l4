# Spec Delta

## Purpose
Figures computed from a cart's line items for sales reports.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Average unit price
`Cart.average_unit_price()` SHALL return the cart subtotal divided by the total quantity of its line items, quantized to 0.01 with ROUND_HALF_UP. For a cart without line items it SHALL raise ValueError.

#### Scenario: Two single items
- **WHEN** the cart holds a line item at 1.00 with quantity 1 and a line item at 4.00 with quantity 1
- **THEN** the average unit price is 2.50

#### Scenario: Quantities are weighted
- **WHEN** the cart holds a line item at 1.00 with quantity 3 and a line item at 5.00 with quantity 1
- **THEN** the average unit price is 2.00

#### Scenario: Half a cent rounds up
- **WHEN** the cart holds a line item at 0.05 with quantity 1 and a line item at 0.00 with quantity 1
- **THEN** the average unit price is 0.03

#### Scenario: Empty cart
- **WHEN** the average unit price of a cart without line items is requested
- **THEN** ValueError is raised

# Answered questions
<l4:data kind="answer" id="Q-001" by="human">
confidence: medium · kind: technical (S1 p=n/a) · needs a human

Return Decimal. Compute subtotal() / Decimal(total quantity), then .quantize(Decimal("0.01"), rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP), the same way Cart.total() does it. Raise ValueError when there are no items. Quantizing with ROUND_HALF_UP only works on Decimal, the subtotal is already a Decimal, and the existing tests compare money with Decimal values. In tests, compare against Decimal("2.50"), Decimal("2.00") and Decimal("0.03").

Citations:
- `specs/cart-stats/spec.md#L9`: "`Cart.average_unit_price()` SHALL return the cart subtotal divided by the total quantity of its line items, quantized to 0.01 with ROUND_HALF_UP."
- `src/pricing/cart.py#L50`: "return max(total, Decimal(0)).quantize(Decimal("0.01"), rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP)"
- `openspec/specs/cart/spec.md#L20`: "The cart SHALL compute its subtotal as the exact Decimal sum of its line totals."
</l4:data>
